# Decisions log

Every preprocessing or modelling choice, with the reason, so it can be defended in the weekly discussions and the technical report.

## 2026-09-30
- Project layout: raw / interim / processed data split so every step can be rerun from the untouched downloads.
- taschenhirn.py: cells with several events are split at paragraph and line breaks into one row per event. Year ranges (e.g. "2010-2012") are kept as text plus `jahr_start` / `jahr_ende` integers.
- Time window: source covers 2000 onward; a cut to 2013 (AfD founding) is a preprocessing option, not applied at scrape time.
- surveys_bund.py: polls are scraped for all institutes listed in the overview table, from the current page back through the yearly archive pages, and concatenated with an added `Institut` column, so columns missing for some institutes or years (e.g. BSW, FW) stay NaN. Only unnamed (spacer) columns are dropped, cell contents stay untouched, and "Bundestagswahl" entries (actual election results, not polls) are excluded. Polls are cut at 2013-02-06 (AfD founding) already at scrape time; changing the window means rerunning the scraper with another `cutoff`.

## 2026-10-01
- surveys_land.py: all Bundesland sections are read from the single overview page and concatenated with an added `Bundesland` column, in the same layout as surveys_bund. The combined cells "Institut (Datum)" and "Befragte / Zeitraum" are split into separate columns, `CDU` and `CSU` are merged into `CDU/CSU`, and the client (`Auftraggeber`) is dropped. `FW` and `BSW` are split out of `Sonstige` into their own columns; all other remaining parties (e.g. PIRATEN) are summed into a single `Sonstige` value, which is left empty if any part is unknown. Party values are read from the right-hand end of each row, because the number of leading cells varies between sections. "Bundestagswahl" rows (actual election results, not polls) are excluded, and, as in surveys_bund, polls are cut at 2013-02-06 (AfD founding) at scrape time.

## 2026-10-06
- btw_wahlkreise.py: official election results by constituency are extracted from bundeswahlleiterin.de for all federal elections from 2013 onward. Party results are mapped into standard groups (CDU/CSU, SPD, Grüne, FDP, Linke, AfD, FW, BSW), while non-group parties and remaining valid votes are aggregated into Sonstige. Non-constituency rows (e.g. state summaries) are excluded to retain strictly constituency-level granularity (299 constituencies per election). Directly elected candidate parties are parsed from the winning column or determined as fallback via the highest first-vote (Erststimmen) count.

## 2026-10-07
- btw_gender_age.py: Results of the representative election statistics: Second votes by gender and age group. Only elections from 2013 onward are kept. `CDU` and `CSU` are summed into `CDU/CSU`, and columns are renamed to the project's standard party names (`Grüne`, `Linke`). Values are shares of second votes in percent. `Geschlecht` is kept as in the source (`m`, `w`, `Summe`; from 2021 `m|d|o`, because the source counts diverse persons and persons without gender entry under men), and the overlapping age groups (`Summe`, `>=60`, `60-69`, `>=70`) are all kept, so rows must not be summed across age groups.
- regierungszufriedenheit.py: government satisfaction ("Wie zufrieden sind Sie mit der Arbeit der Bundesregierung?") is taken from the ARD DeutschlandTrend PDF reports, from the February 2013 edition onward. Because the PDF layout changed several times since 2013 (portrait/landscape, rotated pages, one or two slides per page), values are located by position: the number directly above each category label is taken as that category's value. PDFs without the question (state polls, special editions) or with only aggregated values (e.g. April 2018) are skipped, and duplicate reports of the same survey are stored once, keyed by the end date of the survey period (`Datum`). For 2013–2015 PDFs where the chart is a raster image, values are reconstructed as "reference edition + changes" from the text deltas, with bar heights measured on the rendered page as fallback and cross-check.

## 2026-10-08
- btw_education.py: vote choice by education group ("Wahlentscheidung in den Bildungsgruppen") is taken from the Forschungsgruppe Wahlen Kurzanalyse PDFs of all federal elections from 2013 onward; PDFs are only downloaded if not already present. PDFs without the chart (2017) are skipped. Because the chart layout differs between elections (left/right page column, party order, colours, set of parties), values are not read in text order but assigned to the coloured bar segment they are printed on, with the colour mapped to a party via the chart legend. Segments too small to carry a printed label, and parties not shown in a chart (e.g. BSW before 2025), are left NaN rather than estimated from bar widths. Party names are mapped to the project's standard names (`Grüne`, `Linke`), the older row labels "Hauptschule" / "Hochschule" are harmonised to "Hauptschulabschluss" / "Hochschulabschluss", and the "Wahlergebnis" reference bar (overall result, not an education group) is dropped. `Datum` is the election date read from the chart's source line. Values are exit-poll percentages as printed in the PDF, mostly rounded to whole numbers, so a row's parties do not always sum to exactly 100.
